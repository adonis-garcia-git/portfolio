## What is GLASS?

**GLASS (Global Leaders and Scholars in STEM)** is an honors program at NYU Tandon that transforms engineering students into globally-minded changemakers. The program is structured around **five core windows**:

1. **Global Competency** -- International experiences that build cross-cultural perspective
2. **Commitment to Service** -- Giving back through community engagement and social impact
3. **Leadership** -- Developing the ability to inspire, organize, and lead
4. **Academic Excellence** -- Achieving distinction in scholarship and innovation
5. **Professional Development** -- Building career-ready skills and industry connections

Scholars receive funding for conferences, study abroad, and professional development. The program culminates in a senior project that connects personal passions to NYU Tandon's Research Areas of Excellence, the UN Sustainable Development Goals, and the NAE Grand Challenges.

## My Mission

Building AI-powered educational tools that adapt to how different students learn -- not just personalized to individuals, but responsive to cultural contexts, language barriers, and resource constraints. The goal is to democratize quality education worldwide so that a student's zip code doesn't determine their access.

> "I see AI and machine learning not just as fascinating technologies, but as powerful tools to bridge global educational gaps. My mission is to leverage these innovations to create a world where quality education is accessible to all, regardless of geography or background."

## Senior GLASS Project: Culturally Adaptive CS Learning Platform

**Status:** In Progress

### The Problem

Students around the world learn differently based on cultural context, language, and available resources -- not because of ability. Current educational technology rarely accounts for these differences.

### The Vision

AI-powered educational tools that adapt not just to individuals but to cultural contexts. Intelligent tutoring systems that understand how different cultures approach problem-solving. Virtual reality classrooms that feel familiar whether you're in New York or New Delhi. Technology that democratizes education without erasing cultural identity.

### Research Areas of Excellence

| Area | Connection |
|---|---|
| **Data Science / AI / Robotics** | AI that adapts to individual learning styles and recognizes engagement patterns across cultural backgrounds. |
| **Emerging Media** | VR/AR making immersive learning borderless and accessible -- virtual labs, multilingual AR overlays. |
| **Communications / IT** | Infrastructure reaching remote areas with stable connections on limited bandwidth. |

### UN Sustainable Development Goals

- **SDG 4: Quality Education** -- Inclusive, equitable quality education and lifelong learning for all.
- **SDG 9: Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure** -- Resilient infrastructure and innovation to advance educational access.
- **SDG 10: Reduced Inequalities** -- Reducing inequality in access to education and technology.

### NAE Grand Challenge

**Advance Personalized Learning**

### Deliverables (Expected Spring 2026)

- GLASS Abstract -- Summary of the journey, research focus, and project impact.
- Final Paper -- Comprehensive documentation including methodology, implementation, user research, and future directions.
